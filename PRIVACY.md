Lindo Engine - Privacy Policy

1. Lindo Engine integrates with Discord Rich Presence. When enabled, the engine sends the current scene name and engine version to the local Discord client.
2. Lindo Engine does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data (such as IP addresses, names, or emails) to external servers.
3. No third-party analytics or tracking systems are used.
